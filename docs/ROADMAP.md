# Roadmap

Where Proiproi is headed. This is a direction, not a promise: plans change as I learn from the people using it. Items are grouped by theme, not by date.

Have an idea or a request? Open an [issue](../../issues).

---

## Now

Getting ready for launch.

- Finishing the MVP on iOS, Android and web
- Polishing the core loop: post a project, discover it, apply, get accepted
- Seeding the platform with real projects across every category
- Testing with a small group of creators before opening up

## Next

What I plan to build right after launch.

**Sharing and invites**
- Shareable links for profiles and projects, so you can send someone straight to your work
- Invite links that bring a specific person into a role or a community

**Staying in the loop**
- Notification settings for every type of alert, including new project files
- Web push notifications

**Getting people in**
- Sign in with Apple
- Smoother onboarding for first-time posters

## Later

Bigger ideas, roughly in order of how much I want them.

**Talking to each other**
- In-app messaging between collaborators. This is the feature I expect to come first, since contacting someone after an accepted application is the biggest gap today
- Community announcements and chat

**Finding the right match**
- Faster, smarter search across projects, roles, communities and people
- Saved searches
- Recommended projects and recommended collaborators
- Bulk invites for creators with lots of roles to fill

**Showing your work**
- Portfolio links and past projects on profiles
- Endorsements from people you've worked with
- Short video introductions on applications

**Richer projects**
- Direct video uploads
- Budgets, timelines and a calendar view
- Community analytics for creators and administrators

## Not planned

Proiproi is for collaboration, so a few things are deliberately left out:

- Business, agency or company accounts
- A paid job board
- Client and contractor hiring
